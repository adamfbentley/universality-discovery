## Identity

The project is the estimation theory of effective descriptions: what a
finite scale window — observer's dataset or physical coupling — can
determine about structure beyond it, priced by declared assumptions. Its
product is an instrument for measuring what assumptions are worth, NOT a
method for measuring exponents at small scales (the pipeline itself proved
the latter cannot be done unconditionally).

## Process rules (learned, binding)

- Gates immutable once committed; failures stand; post-hoc analysis in a
  separate section; corrected criteria are preregistered into the NEXT run.
- Executor/reviewer separation; reviewers do not relabel.
- Single-agent blinding is a discipline device, not information isolation;
  say so in reports.
- No novelty claim in any manuscript without a completed, logged
  literature pass (queries + dates + not-found statements).
- Prefer existing validated software (e.g. Kolesár's honest-CI packages)
  over reimplementation; reimplementation caused two failed gate rounds.
- Tool-safety framing: the instrument's failure under a false declaration
  is silent and catastrophic (BD half-window, pending diagnosis). Default
  outputs are curves over U and identified sets, never a single interval.

## Kill criteria

- exp85c confirmatory gate fails against its own analytic prediction →
  instrument claim dies; project contracts to impossibility + identified
  sets (still publishable).
- Literature audit finds the lemma or the FSS optimal-recovery application
  already published → reframe as application/synthesis; drop priority
  language everywhere.
- Ising anchor shows floors so conservative they explain nothing real →
  framework survives only as sensitivity analysis; Tier 1 (a)–(c) still
  proceed.
