## 3. Literature map (my own passes; verdicts + query log)

Verdict labels: FOUND-DIRECT (exists, close match) / FOUND-ADJACENT (neighbouring,
not exact) / NOT-FOUND / NEEDS-LIBRARY (book/offline).

### 3.1 Optimal recovery / honest CIs — FOUND-DIRECT (machinery), FOUND-ADJACENT (FSS application)

The floor *is* an instance of Donoho (1994) modulus-of-continuity optimal recovery;
Armstrong–Kolesár (2018) is the finite-sample fixed-design honest-CI form (with
RDHonest software). Confirmed independently. **The project has correctly retired the
priority claim already.** Novelty that survives: (i) the FSS/corrections-to-scaling
*application*; (ii) the physically-motivated adversary class + computed floors at
real designs; (iii) the closed-form exponential-sum modulus with an RG dictionary;
(iv) the amortized declared-prior pairing. For CIs specifically, the A–K construction
is *exact* (no ≈1.25 constant — that belongs to squared-error estimation), so the
right deliverable is exact honest CIs ("FSSHonest"), which is exp86 Task 3, unbuilt.
- Queries (2026-07-06): "modulus of continuity linear functional exponential sum
  class closed form Donoho optimal recovery corrections to scaling" → Donoho 1994
  (projecteuclid/Berkeley TR), optimal-recovery-for-operators papers; no FSS
  application surfaced.

### 3.2 Tail-index minimax under second-order regular variation — FOUND-ADJACENT — **TOP priority-attack surface**

This is the nearest exponent-estimation precedent and it is real and active:
Hall–Welsh (1984) minimax rate n^{ρ/(1−2ρ)}; Drees (1998, 2001) sharp/adaptive
minimax; **Novak (2014, arXiv:1403.3278) gives finite-sample two-point lower bounds
for tail-index** — structurally the same tool as the project's Le Cam two-point
floor; plus 2025 non-asymptotic adaptive work (arXiv:2505.22371). Stripped of
vocabulary, tail-index estimation = "leading power law + bounded second-order
correction of unknown amplitude/rate, estimate the exponent," which an
extreme-value-statistics referee will read as this project's problem in a change of
clothes.
- **My verdict:** the "why FSS ≠ tail-index" distinction is *defensible but must be
  argued in a dedicated paragraph, not a citation.* The real differences: (a) **fixed
  deterministic small design** (7 sizes, seed-averaged Gaussian) vs random iid
  heavy-tailed sample; (b) a **declared parametric bounded-exponential-sum correction
  class** vs asymptotic second-order-regular-variation / von-Mises *conditions*;
  (c) a **finite-sample fixed-design floor** vs an asymptotic minimax *rate*. Novak
  shows the finite-sample two-point move itself is not new — so the novelty must rest
  on the design+class+computation, not on "we did a finite-sample Le Cam bound."
  This is the single most likely "you missed prior art" attack, *ahead* of the Donoho
  point (which the paper now handles). Read Hall–Welsh, Drees, Novak directly before
  submission.
- Queries: "minimax lower bound tail index estimation second-order regular variation
  Hall Welsh Drees"; "Novak lower bounds accuracy inference heavy tails second order
  finite sample tail index".

### 3.3 Modulus-lemma novelty (bounded-coefficient exponential sums) — FOUND-ADJACENT / plausibly novel — NEEDS-LIBRARY

The machinery for the *lower bound* exists and is exactly what the note names:
Borwein–Erdélyi Bernstein/Markov/Remez/Nikolskii-type inequalities for exponential
sums and Müntz systems (1996–2000), Littlewood-type bounded-coefficient problems on
[0,1] (Borwein–Erdélyi–Kós 1999), Newman's inequality; Müntz–Szász density; the two
2026 "Müntz–Szász network" preprints (arXiv 2601.22751 / 2602.08419) connect scaling
exponents to learnable power bases. But the **specific closed form** — best L²
distance from a *linear target* Δα·x to *bounded-coefficient* N-term exponential sums
on a finite window, = c_N√T·U·(ΔαT/U)^{N+1} — **remains unlocated.**
- **My verdict:** safe wording is the project's own — "adjacent approximation-theory
  results exist; the exact bounded-coefficient finite-window linear-target modulus is
  unlocated in the searched literature." The library-grade pass through the
  Borwein–Erdélyi *book* (not fetchable here) is genuinely owed before any novelty
  language. Independent note: c₁=0.0375 and N+1=2 are numerically certified (I
  reproduced both); the analytic optimality of c_N is open but demoted to enrichment
  by the Donoho hull-sharpness route, which is the correct call.
- Queries: "best approximation linear function by exponential sums bounded coefficients
  Borwein Erdelyi Muntz rate theorem" → Borwein–Erdélyi corpus (adjacent), no exact match.

### 3.4 Learned summaries / SBI — FOUND-ADJACENT

Nuisance handling in SBI is mature but under a *nominal model / sampled prior*, not a
strict worst-case over a declared class: IMNN (Charnock–Lavaux–Wandelt), neural
approximate sufficient statistics, nuisance-hardened compression (Alsing–Wandelt),
**INFERNO / inference-aware neural optimisation (de Castro–Dorigo, arXiv:1806.04743)**,
sensitivity-aware amortized BI, OASIS, the Cranmer–Brehmer–Louppe review.
- **My verdict:** the exp76 amortized estimator is a *declared-prior (average-case)*
  method and is well-precedented — present it as such, not as novel machinery. The
  genuinely open gap is the *forward* idea: **strict minimax training of summaries
  against a declared, structured nuisance class.** INFERNO is the closest occupied
  neighbour and is *not currently cited* in the Masters proposal — it must be, and
  out-positioned (inference-aware under a nominal model ≠ worst-case over an ambiguity
  set). The gap is real but narrow; argue it explicitly.
- Queries: "learned summary statistics minimax worst-case nuisance class simulation-based
  inference neural sufficient statistics 2025 2026".

### 3.5 Forward project — DRO / IRM / PRH / symbolic regression — MIXED

"Structured ambiguity sets for DRO" is **already a named line** (arXiv:2310.20657) —
but its *structure* is statistical (independence across low-dimensional components for
faster shrinkage), **not** physically-interpretable RG-operator sets. Wasserstein/f-
divergence DRO (Sinha; Duchi–Namkoong), IRM/OOD (Arjovsky), PRH (Huh et al.), PySR/
AI-Feynman all exist.
- **My verdict:** the pivot is defensible, but the novelty must be narrowed to
  **"RG irrelevant-operator family as the ambiguity set" + "exact information-ceiling
  testbed"** — do *not* claim "structured ambiguity sets" as a novel phrase (taken).
  PRH is observational; a testbed that makes it "provable or precisely falsifiable" is
  a real contribution but PhD-sized (correctly out of the Masters scope).
- Queries: "distributionally robust optimization structured interpretable ambiguity
  sets representation learning Platonic representation hypothesis".

### 3.6 Physics — FSS corrections / Binder-cumulant route / info-geometric RG — FOUND-DIRECT — **decisive for the anchor**

The standard Binder-cumulant FSS form is g = Q(tL^{1/ν}; L^{−ω}), and the
**temperature-derivative at criticality scales as dU_L/dT|_{T_c} ∝ L^{1/ν}.** That is
a **native per-L affine ladder**: log(dU_L/dT|_{T_c}) = const + (1/ν)·log L +
(bounded corrections) — *exactly the project's normal form*, with 1/ν as the tilt.
This is the native-affine Ising observable the project has been missing; exp52d's PC1
collapse is a *global* collapse-quality minimizer, which is why it does not define a
per-L affine ladder and why the reconstructed proxy fails to track ν. Info-geometric
RG (Bény–Osborne) is real but supplies no finite-window floor.
- **My verdict:** this converts the anchor from "blocked/failing" to "mis-instrumented,"
  and prescribes the fix (§4 Path C). This is my highest-value independent finding.
- Queries: "Binder cumulant finite-size scaling slope derivative L^{1/nu} critical
  exponent extraction method".
